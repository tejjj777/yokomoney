Privacy Policy

YOKO! Student by YOKOMONEY · Last updated 12 October 2026

The short version.

Your money data lives on your device. If you turn on sync, it is locked with your passphrase before it leaves your device, so we can’t read it.

We never sell your data, show ads or share it for marketing. Our visitor counter uses no cookies.

Who we are

YOKO! Student (“YOKO!”, “we”) is a free money planner for students, run by YOKOMONEY. Our support email is being set up and will be listed here soon.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (allowance, income, spends, budgets, goals, roommates, notes and settings) is saved in your browser’s storage on your device. It doesn’t leave your device unless you use one of the features below. Receipts, bill photos and bank messages you paste are read on your device too.

Clearing your browser data, or using “Reset all data” in Settings, deletes it. Back it up first with the export or backup options in Settings.

Budget alerts and reminders are made on your device. We don’t send push notifications from a server.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell, rent or trade your data.
  • We don’t show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We don’t connect to your bank. You choose what to type, paste or scan.
  • We can’t read your synced data. If you lose both your passphrase and your recovery key, we can’t recover it for you.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your device: until you delete it.
  • Your account and synced copy: until you delete your account (Settings → Sync across devices → Delete account). This removes both straight away.
  • Live group data: until the expenses are deleted in the group. To remove a whole group, contact us.
  • AI requests: our function doesn’t store them.
  • Visit counts: kept as totals.

Your rights

You can see, export, correct and delete your data in the app at any time. You can also contact us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We aim to reply within 7 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact the data protection authority where you live (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).

Children

YOKO! is made for students. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian’s permission, especially before turning on sync or live groups.

Security

The app is served only over HTTPS. Sync uses AES-256 encryption with a key made from your passphrase on your device. Database rows are locked so each person can only reach their own. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your passphrase and recovery key private.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top and mention big changes in the app’s “What’s new”.
